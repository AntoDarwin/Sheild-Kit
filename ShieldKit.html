    What ShieldKit is

    ShieldKit is a small awareness toolkit for the "Hack the Hackers" theme. It helps ordinary people detect phishing links, prevent weak passwords, respond after a hit, and learn the tricks scammers use.

    Detect

    Scores a link against known phishing habits.

    Prevent

    Tests passwords and makes strong ones.

    Respond

    A clear order of steps after an attack.

    Learn

    Eight short scam scenarios with answers.

    Why it is not AI

    Nothing here learns, guesses or generates answers. Each result comes from rules a person wrote down and you can read.

    • The link checker adds points for fixed warning signs, such as http instead of https, a raw IP address, an @ in the address, look-alike letters, or words like "verify" and "login".
    • The password tester counts character types and length, checks a short list of common passwords and simple patterns, then does the arithmetic for how many guesses it would take.
    • The same input always gives the same answer. There is no model, no training data and no server. The page works offline once loaded.
    • Your links and passwords stay in this page and are gone when you close it.
    What it cannot do
    • It cannot look up whether a site is on a live blocklist, so a low score is not a promise that a link is safe.
    • Its password time estimate assumes a thief with a stolen database and fast hardware. Real attacks vary.
    • When a message feels wrong, trust that and contact the company through its app or a number you already have.
    ShieldKit runs entirely in this page. Rule-based, no AI.
